# Shelf Shepherd — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Shelf Shepherd is a household food-tracking app for Android and iPhone. It is
designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We, the makers of Shelf Shepherd, never receive your data unless you
email us (see **Feedback you send**). The app has no user accounts, no
analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

A few optional features contact another service to do their job. Each one is
off until you turn it on, sends only what is described below, and can be
turned off again at any time in **Settings**.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter — your food inventory, shopping list, savings history,
and preferences — is stored locally on your device in the app's private
storage. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data under
that service's terms; a saved key is excluded from Android backups.

You can export all of your data as a readable file, or permanently delete all
of it, at any time from **Settings** inside the app.

## What runs on your phone

- **Notifications (optional):** reminders you turn on, such as "this expires
  this week." They are scheduled on your phone; you choose how often and when.
- **Camera and photos:** barcode scanning, receipt scanning, date-label
  reading, and food recognition run on your device using built-in models. The
  live camera view is analyzed as you scan and never saved. Photos you take or
  pick are deleted from the app's cache as soon as the items are extracted.
- **Your phone's built-in AI:** on phones that offer it (Gemini Nano on
  Android, Apple Intelligence on iPhone), Shelf Shepherd asks it to help name foods
  and expand receipt abbreviations. It runs on the phone; Shelf Shepherd sends
  nothing off the device to use it.

## Optional features that use the internet

- **Barcode lookup (Settings → Scanning & AI, off by default):** when you scan
  a product the app doesn't know yet, only the barcode number is sent to
  [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy), a free public
  product database, to find the product's name. The answer is saved on your
  phone so the same barcode is never looked up twice.
- **Claude AI with your own key (Settings → Advanced):** if you add your own
  Anthropic API key, Snap Food photos are sent over an encrypted connection
  directly to Anthropic's Claude API to identify the foods, and are then
  handled under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy).
  The app keeps neither the photo nor the response beyond your review screen.
  The key is stored encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware
  and is sent only to Anthropic. Remove the key to turn the feature off.

## Feedback you send

**Settings → Send feedback** opens your own email app with a message addressed
to us; nothing is sent unless you press Send there. The email holds what you
write, the topic you pick, any screenshots or photos you choose to attach
(re-saved without location or camera details) and, unless you switch it off,
the app version, phone model, system version and whether you use Free, Trial
or Premium. It never includes anything about your food or kitchen. We use
feedback emails only to reply to you and to improve the app.

## External links

Meal ideas may offer an optional "search the web" link, and the shopping list
can open another shopping app after you copy the list. Sites and apps you open
this way have their own privacy policies; Shelf Shepherd sends them nothing.

## Estimates, not guarantees

Savings amounts are estimates based on values you enter. Suggested expiration
dates are general guidance and never a food-safety guarantee — always check
the package and use your judgment.

## Children

Shelf Shepherd is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect any
data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, adding
purchases), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the
in-app description of the feature will explain exactly what is sent.
